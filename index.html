About vyud.ai

Engineering the Future of Financial Technology

vyud.ai Technologies is an AI-first technology company committed to building the technologies of tomorrow. We combine deep expertise in fintech, cloud-native engineering, artificial intelligence, and enterprise software to help organizations transform how they operate.

As an Official Mifos Partner, we specialize in implementing and supporting Apache Fineract and Mifos Payment Hub, enabling financial institutions to deliver secure, scalable, and inclusive digital banking services.

Beyond banking, our subscription-based SaaS products empower businesses with intelligent tools for analytics, security, project delivery, asset tracking, and operational management. Our goal is simple: use technology to improve organizations, expand financial inclusion, and positively impact thousands of lives.
